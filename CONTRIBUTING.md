# Contributing papers

Please add one record to the canonical bibliography and include:

- Exact paper title and author list
- Final publication year and venue, with online-first year separately when different
- Publication type: journal, magazine, conference, workshop or preprint
- DOI and an official publisher or author/institutional metadata link
- Authorized open-access version, when available; do not upload publisher PDFs without permission
- Primary category and direct-WLAN versus transferable-MEC/edge-AI relevance
- One original sentence explaining how the work helps Wi-Fi AI offload research
- One relevant scope or evidence limitation
- Date metadata was checked

Use the final journal record when verified. Do not create a separate duplicate for its arXiv access copy. Conference and extended journal versions can both be included if their relationship and additional contribution are made clear.

Do not infer publication year from the DOI string. Do not claim a paper proposes an adopted IEEE 802.11bu mechanism unless an independently verified standards record supports that claim. Keep standards contributions and SG straw polls outside the peer-reviewed paper count.

For standards documents, preserve the exact document number, revision and status. A later revision may change the proposal. Describe empirical gains only with the original measurement conditions and baseline.
